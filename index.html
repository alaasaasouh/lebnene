Founded 2008 · Beirut → World

A Family's Dream, A City's Heritage

What began as a family table in Hamra has grown into a restaurant celebrated for keeping Lebanese culinary traditions alive with modern elegance.

02

Executive Chef · Nour Khalil

Trained in Beirut & Lyon

Chef Nour bridges classical French technique with the soul of Lebanese cooking — sumac, za'atar, and seven spices reimagined for the modern palate.

03

Sourcing · Bekaa Valley

Imported Spices, Local Produce

We import spice blends directly from the Bekaa Valley while sourcing vegetables and dairy from local farms within 50 miles. Zero compromise on quality.

04

Recognition · Awards

Michelin Bib Gourmand 2023–2024

Recognised for exceptional quality and outstanding value. Featured in Time Out, Condé Nast Traveller, and Gourmet Magazine Arabia.
